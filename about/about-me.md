## About Me

I am an aspiring cybersecurity professional with a strong interest in defensive security, investigations, and threat analysis.

I hold a higher education degree in Law and have over 7 years of experience in investigative roles within the National Police of Ukraine. For 5 years, I worked as a criminal investigator, and later for 2 years in an internal investigations department, where I conducted internal reviews and personnel-related investigations.

I decided to leave law enforcement due to systemic limitations that prevented me from fully realizing myself as a professional investigator. At the same time, I pursued a long-standing goal of opening my own confectionery business, which I continue to manage remotely in Ukraine.

My interest in cybersecurity began after completing an introductory cybersecurity course on Coursera. I recognized strong parallels between digital investigations and traditional investigative work.

In spring 2026, due to the war in Ukraine, I relocated with my family to Costa Rica.

Currently, I am focusing on:
- Cybersecurity fundamentals
- Hands-on labs (TryHackMe)
- Linux basics

This portfolio documents my learning process as I prepare for an entry-level role in cybersecurity.
